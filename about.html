About

Why we built The Mridangam Project

We started as a small reference site for people curious about the mridangam, and grew into a place to also buy well-made instruments and parts — without losing the reference material that got us here.

What this site is

The Mridangam Project is a single destination that explains what the mridangam is, why it matters in Carnatic music, how it's built, and who mastered it — alongside a small shop for instruments and parts, built by people who care about the craft as much as the commerce.

Every product listed here is described honestly, including trade-offs, so you can make an informed choice whether you're a first-time student or a touring professional.

Our approach

  • Clear, jargon-explained information first
  • Honest product descriptions, including trade-offs
  • Support for players at every level, from student to concert artist
