Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering, shipping, sizing, and looking after your instrument.

Ordering & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

This is a demo storefront, so the checkout offers UPI, card, and cash-on-delivery as selectable options, but no real payment is processed.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

On a real store, orders can typically be changed within a short window after placement — contact support as soon as possible via the About/Contact page.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No — you can add items to your cart and check out as a guest. Creating an account just lets you view your order history later from the same browser.

Shipping

How long does delivery take within India?

Typical delivery windows for musical instruments like this run 5–10 business days depending on location, with metro cities usually faster.

Is shipping free?

Orders over ₹5,000 ship free; smaller orders (mostly individual parts) carry a flat shipping fee, shown at checkout before you pay.

Do you ship internationally?

Not directly through this storefront today — for international orders, see the sellers listed on our Buying Guide page, several of whom ship worldwide.

Choosing & Caring for a Mridangam

Which size should a beginner buy?

Most teachers recommend a student-grade, full or semi-concert size instrument once a learner has outgrown a mini/practice size — see our Anatomy & Craft page for how sizing affects tone.

How do I care for the drumheads?

Keep the instrument away from direct sun and extreme humidity swings, wipe the thoppi paste off after each session, and store it on a stand or padded case rather than flat on the floor.

What's the difference between the mridangams and the parts listed in the shop?

Complete mridangams are full instruments ready to play; parts are individual replacement components (heads, straps, tuning blocks, etc.) for repairing or upgrading an instrument you already own. Both categories are browsable from the Shop page.
